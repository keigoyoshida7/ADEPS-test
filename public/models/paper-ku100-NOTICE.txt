SHA256: e6c72a84dd947b5ef75438ab96a9c2a32ed10f033472b9c4c11a49aff00a8a31
Source data: 8802 directions, 2 ears, 256 samples at 48000 Hz.
Source file history includes low-frequency extension, diffuse-field
equalization and windowing. These are processed measured HRTFs, not raw
uncompensated measurements.

Changes made for ADEPS-test:
1. Spherical Voronoi-area-weighted least-squares projection into real,
   first-order ACN/N3D coefficients [W,Y,Z,X].
2. Evaluation of the projected 48 kHz filters at the 16 kHz / FFT512
   frequency grid, 0–8000 Hz in 31.25 Hz steps, using their DTFT.
3. Real DC and Nyquist coefficients for compatibility with a real 16 kHz
   waveform. No model, scene, result or test score is used to fit this decoder.

Rebuild with the checksum-pinned source file, NumPy, SciPy and h5py:
python backend/paper_binaural_metrics.py prepare --sofa PATH_TO_SOURCE_SOFA

This is an independently chosen first-order decoder for supplementary
reference-based binaural metrics. The original ADEPS article does not identify
the precise KU100 measurement file or decoder configuration; this asset is
not claimed to be that article’s original renderer. No endorsement by the
